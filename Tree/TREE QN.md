|Pattern|Problems|
|---|---|
|DFS|Height, Diameter, Path Sum|
|BFS|Level Order, Zigzag, Right View|
|Recursion|Almost every tree problem|
|Backtracking|Path Sum II|
|HashMap|Prefix Sum, Vertical Order|
|Queue|BFS|
|Stack|Iterative Traversals|
|Parent Mapping|Distance K, Burn Tree|
|DP on Trees|House Robber III|
|BST Property|Validate, Delete, Kth Smallest|
